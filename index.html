About Me

My Development During the BTS

Over the course of the BTS Cloud Computing programme, I have grown significantly both technically and personally. Starting with a basic understanding of networks and operating systems, I quickly built confidence working with Linux servers — setting up services, managing users, and hardening systems against common threats. Throughout the two years, I tackled real-world projects that pushed me to think critically, work in a team, and deliver results under pressure. Whether it was configuring a virtualised infrastructure from scratch, writing automation scripts, or troubleshooting complex network issues, every challenge sharpened my skills and deepened my passion for IT. This programme has turned me from a curious student into someone ready to contribute professionally in a cloud and infrastructure environment.
